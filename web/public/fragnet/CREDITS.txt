FRAGNET bundled asset pack (pack.json)
=====================================

The textures, flats, sprites, PLAYPAL and COLORMAP in pack.json are derived
from Freedoom 1 (v0.12.1), the free (BSD-3-Clause) replacement assets for the
classic corridor-shooter engine. Freedoom is by the Freedoom project,
https://freedoom.github.io/

The pack is a build-time extraction (scripts/mkfragnetpack.mjs) of only the
lumps FRAGNET draws. No DOOM, DOOM II or id Software asset is bundled or
shipped: the original WADs are commercial and stay that way. To play FRAGNET
with your own legally-owned DOOM.WAD / DOOM1.WAD / freedoom2.wad, upload it
from the FRAGNET panel; it is parsed live in the browser and never leaves the
LAN relay.
